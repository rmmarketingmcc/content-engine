# Story seeds

Stories told on calls that have not been posted yet. The drafter reads this file with --seeds and the scout skips
stories already here. One numbered line per seed: "N. **Title** what happened (source, date). Check: what to confirm".
A seed tagged **[Proposed by the author]** is one the author asked for: it gets a post first (the tag is [drafting]
proposed_tag in config.toml). The seeds below are made-up EXAMPLES that show the format: delete them when you start.

1. **EXAMPLE: The quote that went out at midnight** A customer asked for a price at 23.40; the quote went out the same night and won the job over two others who answered the next day (interviewer call, 03 Feb, 06:10). Check: the customer's OK to tell it without a name.
2. **[Proposed by the author] EXAMPLE: Why I stopped offering a cheap package** The smallest package took the most time and brought the fewest referrals (note, 05 Feb). Check: the share of time, in percent.
3. **EXAMPLE: The form nobody saw** A contact form sent its mails to an old address for four months (interviewer call, 10 Feb, 14:22). Check: nothing, the author's own site.

## Privacy
- Never a client's name, town or anything that points to one client without their OK.
