# Who the author wants as clients

The drafter and the scout write for these readers. Internal: its counts and open questions never go into a post.
Everything below is a made-up EXAMPLE: replace it with your own.

## In one line
- EXAMPLE: Danish craft businesses with 2 to 20 employees that get most of their work from calls and quote requests.

## Who we want
- EXAMPLE: owners who answer their own phone and lose jobs because quotes go out late.

## Who we don't want
- EXAMPLE: webshops, chains, anyone who wants the cheapest price above all.

## Referrers
- EXAMPLE: accountants and suppliers who know many craft businesses.
