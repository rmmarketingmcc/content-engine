# The rules the engine follows

Every rule has a source. The post writer, the graphics designer and the interviewer read this file on every batch;
content_engine_feedback.py adds new rules under the right heading from the author's own words (a mail reply, a call,
a hand edit in the doc). Keep the six headings: the feedback step only writes under these. The lines below are made-up
EXAMPLES that show the format: delete them when you start.

## What counts
- EXAMPLE: DMs count, likes don't: a post is good when a business owner recognises their own situation and writes: "det er beskederne, der tæller" (mail reply, 03 Feb).

## Tone of voice
- EXAMPLE: Never "kære læser" or any greeting to the reader: "ingen hilsner, bare start" (edit in the doc, 05 Feb).

## Posting
- EXAMPLE: A post about a client result names the trade, never the town: "byen afslører dem" (review call, 07 Feb).

## Facts, privacy and money
- EXAMPLE: Prices only as "fra X kr.", never a full price list: "priserne skifter" (note, 10 Feb).

## Images and video
- EXAMPLE: No stock photos, ever: "kun rigtige billeder af mig" (mail reply, 12 Feb).
