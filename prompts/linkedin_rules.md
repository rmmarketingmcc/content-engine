# House rules for LinkedIn posts

The drafter reads this file before every batch. It holds your standing rules; the rules the engine learns from your
feedback go to data/kb/voice_rules.md instead. Edit freely: everything below is a starting point, not anyone's real
rulebook.

## Formatting
- Short lines, one thought per line, a blank line between short paragraphs.
- No hashtags, no bullet points, no long dashes.
- 120 to 260 words. The first two or three lines must work on their own: they are all that shows before "...se mere".

## Post types to rotate
- Story: a specific moment, what happened, what it cost or brought, what you learned.
- Numbers: one real number from your own work, what it means for the reader, what they can check in their own business.
- Contrarian: a common belief in your trade, why you disagree, one example.
- Quick insight: one short observation, one question back.

## Hooks that tend to work
- A specific moment ("Kl. 22.14 ringede en kunde ...").
- A number that surprises.
- A claim the reader disagrees with at first.

## Endings
- A real question the reader can answer from their own experience, or a soft one-line offer (a free guide, a free check).
- Never "Del gerne", never a list of ways to work with you.

## Privacy
- Never a client's name, or a niche plus a detail that points to one client, without their written OK.
- No client revenue, profit or budget in kroner. Percentages are fine.
